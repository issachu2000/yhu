ABOUT

Research at the intersection of data, economics, and development.

I am a Junior Professional Associate in the World Bank Group’s Chief Economist Office for Asia. My research interests include artificial intelligence and labor markets, international trade, climate resilience, and firm behavior in developing economies.

My recent work uses job postings, labor force surveys, firm-level data, geospatial data, and large language models to measure technology exposure, study labor demand and firm behavior, and construct new indicators at scale.

I have contributed to World Bank flagship research and policy publications, working papers, and academic research outputs.
