About

I am a Junior Professional Associate in the World Bank Group’s Chief Economist Office for Asia Pacific. My research interests include artificial intelligence and labor markets, international trade, climate resilience, and firm behavior in developing economies.

I use large-scale firm, labor market, survey, and geospatial data to study how technological change and economic shocks affect firms and workers. My recent research focuses on measuring AI exposure and technology adoption using job postings, labor force surveys, firm-level data, and large language models.

Selected Work

From Risk to Resilience
World Bank flagship report on climate resilience and development in South Asia.

AI and Labor Markets
Research using large-scale job postings, labor force surveys, firm-level data, and LLM-based methods to study AI exposure and technology adoption.

Publications and Reports

My work has contributed to World Bank flagship research and policy publications, including From Risk to Resilience, multiple editions of the South Asia Development Update, and research supporting the World Development Report 2026.
