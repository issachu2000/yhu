About

I am a Junior Professional Associate in the World Bank Group’s Chief Economist Office for Asia. My recent work combines large-scale administrative, labor-market, firm-level, survey, and geospatial data with empirical methods and large language models.

I have contributed to World Bank research and flagship publications on artificial intelligence, labor markets, trade, climate resilience, and firms in developing economies.
