About

I am a Junior Professional Associate in the World Bank Group’s Chief Economist Office for Asia. My research interests include artificial intelligence and labor markets, international trade, climate resilience, and firm behavior in developing economies.

I use large-scale firm, labor market, survey, and geospatial data to study how technological change and economic shocks affect firms and workers. My recent research focuses on measuring AI exposure and technology adoption using job postings, labor force surveys, firm-level data, and large language models.

Selected Research

From Risk to Resilience

Climate resilience and development in South Asia. I served as Head RA and project coordinator, coordinating the research team and supporting the report’s data analysis, visualization, and production.

Artificial Intelligence and Labor Markets

Research using large-scale job postings, labor force surveys, firm-level datasets, and large language models to study AI exposure, technology adoption, and labor demand.

Trade and Firms

Empirical research on trade, tariffs, global value chains, and firm behavior in developing economies.

Publications & Reports

My work has contributed to World Bank flagship research and policy publications, including From Risk to Resilience, multiple editions of the South Asia Development Update, and research supporting the World Development Report 2026.
